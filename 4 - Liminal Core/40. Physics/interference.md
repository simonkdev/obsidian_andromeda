### General:
If two waves meet each other (= overlap in space and time), for example due to [[diffraction]], they interfere with each other. In spots where peaks and valleys cross paths, we get destructive interference because they even out each other's signals. In spots where peaks line up with peaks and valleys line up with valleys, we get constructive interference because they create new waves with more intensity (two peaks lining up form a higher valley than before) .
