### General:
The Bell Circuit consists of a Hadamard Gate, whose input is funneled into a CNOT Gate alongside a second input.  

### The Bell Basis:
The results of the circuit can be summarised in four cases:
$$\displaylines{
B(\ket{00}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}\ket{00} + \frac{1}{\sqrt2}\ket{11}
\newline
B(\ket{01}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}\ket{01} + \frac{1}{\sqrt2}\ket{10}
\newline 
B(\ket{10})= \frac{1}{\sqrt2}\ket{00} - \frac{1}{\sqrt2}\ket{11}
\newline 
B(\ket{11}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}\ket{01} - \frac{1}{\sqrt2}\ket{10}
}$$
These results are, of course entangled. They also form the so-called Bell Basis.
