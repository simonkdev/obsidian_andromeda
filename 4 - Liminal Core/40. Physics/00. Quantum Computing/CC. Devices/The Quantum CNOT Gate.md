### General:
The Quantum CNOT Gate is used to entangle two electrons/photons. It is defined as follows:
$$CNOT(r\ket{00} + s \ket{01} + t \ket{10} + u\ket{11}) = r\ket{00} + s \ket{01} + u \ket{10} + t\ket{11}$$
Note that it swaps $u$ and $t$, therefore exchanging the probability amplitudes of $\ket{10}$ and $\ket{11}$ and flipping the state of the second qubit.
*This action also entangles the qubits.*
