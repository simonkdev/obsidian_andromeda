### General:
The most important single-bit quantum gate by far. It is often used to put standard vectors into superpositions:
$$ \displaylines {
H(\ket{0}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}(\ket{0} + \ket{1})
\newline
H(\ket{1}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}(\ket{0} - \ket{1})
}$$
It is represented by this matrix:
$$H = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt2} & \frac{1}{\sqrt2}  \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt2}  & -\frac{1}{\sqrt2} \end{bmatrix}$$
