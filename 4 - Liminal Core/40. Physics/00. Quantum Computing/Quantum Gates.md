### General:
Quantum gates influence the state qubits passing through them. They can be represented by a matrix and applying them is mathematically equal to a matrix multiplication of the current state vector by the matrix of the gate.
They can be used to perform operations analogous to classical bits.

### Notation:
We will denote a gate like a function, e.g. $X(\ket{0})$ or like a multiplication, e.g. $X \ket{0}$.

### Important gates:
The most important gates are  [[The Quantum CNOT Gate]], [[The Hadamard Gate]] and the [[Pauli transforms]].
