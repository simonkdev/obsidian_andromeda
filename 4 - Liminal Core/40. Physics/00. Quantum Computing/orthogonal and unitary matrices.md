### General:
A matrix is orthogonal if it is a square matrix and the equation $M^TM = I_n$ is true. 
	(see section 5 of [[matrices]])
Unitary is the same but for if the matrix contains complex numbers. Since real numbers are a subset of complex numbers, all the ones we will see at first will be unitary as well.
