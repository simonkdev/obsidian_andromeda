### General:
Like in superdense coding, sender and receiver have one electron of an entangled pair of electrons. Additionally, the sender has another electron in an unknown state which they want to copy to the receiver's electron.

The sender will act on their two qubits using the inverse Bell Circuit (see [[The Bell Circuit and Bell Basis]]) and then measure the entangled one. 
This will cause the receiver's qubit to jump to one of four variations of the additional electron. 
Using the result of the sender, which is transmitted to the receiver seperately, they decide on which of the [[Pauli transforms]] to apply to get the same state of the initial electron.

Since additional info has to be transmitted seperately, this can not happen faster than the speed of light.
