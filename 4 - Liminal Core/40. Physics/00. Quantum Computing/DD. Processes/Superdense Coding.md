### General:
For Superdense Coding, the sender and receiver both possess one of two entangled electrons.
The sender then passes their electron through one of the [[Pauli transforms]], according to the two classical bits they want to transmit, and send their electron to the receiver. Once they measure the sent qubit to receive the first bit, their own changes to the state corresponding to the second bit.
Since we used one qubit to transmit two classical bits, we call it **Superdense (En)Coding**.
