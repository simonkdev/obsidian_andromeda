### General:
If we use any quantum gate with qubits either in state $\ket{0}$ or $\ket{1}$ and let them represent bit values according to their name, they behave exactly like normal gates, meaning using qubits does not restrict us from doing regular Computation. Therefore, Quantum Computation includes all of Classical Computation.
