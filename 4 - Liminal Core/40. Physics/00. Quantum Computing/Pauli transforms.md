### General:
The pauli transforms refer to a group of four [[Quantum Gates]].

### The Gate I:
This is just the identity matrix $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$. It acts as the identity and leaves any qubit passing through completely unchanged.

### The Gate Z:
This is given by the matrix $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{bmatrix}$. It changes the relative phase of the passing qubit (meaning it changes the sign of one of the probability amplitudes), which does not have an effect on the basis vectors, but every qubit is affected by it. 

### The Gate X:
This is represented in the matrix  $\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$ and corresponds to the NOT gate in classical computation. It interchanges the basis vectors in which the qubits are represented., flipping the current state of the electron or photon.

### The Gate Y: 
This is a combination of Gates Z and X. It flips the basis vectors and changes the probability amplitude of one of them. It is represented by $\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$.
