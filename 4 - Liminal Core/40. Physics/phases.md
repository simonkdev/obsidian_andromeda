### General:
Phases describe at which point of the  oscillation cycle a wave is.  If you freeze a wave (for example, an electromagnetic wave of light) in any point in time or measure it as it passes through a specific point, the phase will describe at which point on the cycle you currently are.

### In science:
Most of the time, when you talk about the phase of e.g. light, you are talking about the phase difference to another wave.
