### General:
Electromagnetic waves (EMW)  or electromagnetic radiation is a wave in the electromagnetic field. A wave functions by particles moving vertically making other particles next to them move vertically as well.

All forms of EMW exhibit [[wave-particle duality]], acting as both particles (photons) and waves.
