##### General:
A gradient is a vector composed of a functions' partial derivatives. Its length is equal to
the dimension count of the function. It is represented with the character $\nabla$.
If you were to plot it as an arrow, it would point into the direction with the highest change and have a magnitude
of the maximum rate of change.
