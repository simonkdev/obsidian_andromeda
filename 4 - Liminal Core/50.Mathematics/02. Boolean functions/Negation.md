### General:
A negation is the inversion of a boolean value. For example, if the boolean statement P is true, its negation is false, and it is the same the other way around.

### Notation:
A negation is denoted with the Prefix $\neg$ in front of the statement name. In the example above, the negation of P would be denoted as $\neg P$.
