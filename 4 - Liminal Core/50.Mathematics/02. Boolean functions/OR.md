### General:
The inclusive or statement is true if at least one of its denoted statements is true. The exclusive or is true if only one of both is true, but not if both are true.


### Notation:
The inclusive and exclusive or are denoted as $\lor$ and $\oplus$ respectively. Here are two examples with the statements P and Q: 
$$ \displaylines{
P \lor Q
\newline
P \oplus Q
}$$
