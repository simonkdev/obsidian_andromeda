### General:
Complex numbers are a field of numbers denoted by $\mathbb{C}$ and expand on the field of real numbers $\mathbb{R}$. Complex matrices and vectors are nothing more than regular matrices that use complex numbers. 

Some algebraic operations are slightly modified, with the [[Hermitian transpose]] replacing the classic transpose and the inner product using the Hermitian transpose instead of the normal one.
